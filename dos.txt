1. Purpose
The Director of Studies (DOS) is the primary academic operational authority within the School Management System.
The DOS is responsible for ensuring that:
Teaching is properly organized
Curriculum is implemented
Teachers are academically coordinated
Timetables are functioning
Syllabus progress is monitored
Student academic attendance is monitored
Examinations are properly managed
Assessment is controlled
Results are processed
Academic performance is monitored
Academic intervention is initiated where required
The DOS reports operationally to the Principal / Head Teacher.

2. Role Philosophy
The DOS's core question is:
Is the academic system functioning correctly, are teachers delivering the required curriculum, and are students achieving the expected academic outcomes?
The DOS is therefore the primary academic authority.
The DOD is responsible for student discipline and operational welfare.
These roles must remain separate.

50. Final DOS Definition
The School Management System should treat the DOS as:
The academic authority responsible for academic planning, curriculum, teaching coordination, teacher academic assignments, timetabling, academic attendance, assessment, examinations, results, student academic performance, syllabus monitoring, and academic intervention.
The DOS should work in coordination with the Principal, DOD, teachers, parents, administration, and other departments while maintaining clear ownership of academic operations.
The DOS role must strengthen the existing School Management System and must not replace or modify unrelated roles and functionality.
